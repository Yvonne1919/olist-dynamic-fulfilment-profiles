## Included / excluded

Included: exact reusable scientific code; necessary predecessor utilities;
frozen scientific settings and selections; existing test definitions; compact
aggregate result/plot tables; final figure builders; selected curated LaTeX
tables; lightweight environment and public-release metadata.

Excluded: all raw Olist files; order/review/entity-level datasets and predictions;
large profile stores; failed-run directories; exploratory notebooks and scratch
analyses; obsolete model experiments except an explicitly required helper;
credentials and tokens; private prompts and notes; manuscript drafts and
annotated PDFs; audio; literature PDFs; research Git history; virtual
environments, caches and build logs; private governance documents and run-time
integrity inventories. Only the two small aggregate confirmation-label tables
are retained from a study's `working/` directory.

The few included source modules for preflight/reporting are required by
existing imports/tests or document original execution logic. Their private
inputs are not bundled and their full-run commands are not claimed to work.
